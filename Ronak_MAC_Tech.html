Introduction

What is IEEE MTT-S?

A quick look at the society that powers innovation in microwave and RF engineering worldwide.

📡

A Global Society

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Techniques Society (MTT-S) is one of the largest and most active societies within IEEE. It brings together engineers, researchers and students working on microwave, millimetre-wave, RF, antenna and photonic technologies.

🔬

Fields of Focus

MTT-S covers the theory, design and application of microwave components, circuits and systems — from 5G/6G communication and radar to satellite links, biomedical sensing and electromagnetic compatibility.

📚

Knowledge & Publications

Members gain access to highly cited journals such as IEEE Transactions on MTT, the IEEE Microwave and Wireless Technology Letters, and the flagship IEEE MTT-S International Microwave Symposium (IMS).

🤝

Why Join?

Access to technical talks, distinguished lecturers, student design competitions, travel grants, networking with industry leaders, and hands-on exposure to RF/microwave design tools and hardware.

About Us

The MITS-DU Gwalior Chapter

A student-led technical community under the IEEE Student Branch of MITS-DU Gwalior, dedicated to building practical RF and microwave engineering skills beyond the classroom.

🎯

Our Mission

To create a strong platform for students of MITS-DU Gwalior to learn, build and innovate in microwave, RF, antenna and wireless domains — bridging the gap between academic curriculum and industry expectations.

🚀

What We Do

We organise hands-on workshops, expert talks, simulation bootcamps, project showcases and industry visits. Members work with tools such as HFSS, CST, ADS, MATLAB and KiCad, and participate in IEEE-level competitions.

👥

Who Can Join

Open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students of MITS-DU Gwalior with an interest in Electronics, Communication, Electrical, Computer or allied engineering streams. No prior RF experience required.

🌟

Our Vision

To establish MITS-DU Gwalior as a recognised student hub for microwave and RF research in the central India region, producing industry-ready engineers and future researchers.
